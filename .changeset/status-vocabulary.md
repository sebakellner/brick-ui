The graph gains a status vocabulary: `success`, `warning` and `info`, each with `bg`, `text`
and `border`, in both themes. Every pair was measured against WCAG before it was chosen, so
`success-text` on `success-bg` reads at 6.49:1 in light and 5.23:1 in dark, and then again on
a plain page surface at 6.54:1 and 10.04:1. The palette steps for these families existed and
painted nothing; they paint now.
