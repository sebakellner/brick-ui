## Invalid, with the error atom

`invalid` is the field's, and the atom only paints the text. The control's
`error` property is what makes the input invalid, so the two are written
together until the form-error model gives the atom a validity source.

<Canvas of={FieldStories.Invalid} />

## Atoms, and the properties they replace

The `label` and `helper` properties are the fallback content of the slots, so
both spellings produce the same field.
